## 7. GTA 6 ONLINE / MOD SUPPORT / ROLEPLAY RELEVANCE

### Online / multiplayer
- **No official GTA 6 online mode announced.** No name, no date, no Newswire post either confirming or denying.
- Rockstar's own marketing language is consistent and deliberate: GTA VI "features a single-player experience set in the biggest, most immersive evolution of the series yet." The PlayStation Store FAQ states "Grand Theft Auto VI is a single-player experience."
- **This means GTA 6 ships Nov 19, 2026 with no online mode.**
- "GTA Online 2" is fan terminology, not official.
- GTA Online (GTA 5) explicitly stays in market.

### Mod support for GTA 6
- **Nothing official.** No statement about PC mods, SDK, UGC tools, or RP support for GTA VI.
- Signal (not a commitment): Rockstar posted a job listing for a **Senior Product Manager for "Rockstar's Creator Platforms"**, covering UGC and RP platforms such as FiveM/RedM. Interpreted by press as building toward official creator tooling.
- Because GTA 6 has **no dated PC version**, any GTA 6 RP scene is gated behind a PC release that does not yet exist. **Practical planning assumption: GTA 6 RP is not viable in 2026 or 2027.** GTA V precedent: console Sep 17, 2013 → PC Apr 14, 2015 (~18.9 months). Same ratio from Nov 19, 2026 → roughly mid-2028.

### Cfx.re / FiveM under Rockstar
- **August 2023**: Rockstar Games announced Cfx.re (FiveM, RedM) is "officially a part of Rockstar Games" via the Newswire post **"Roleplay Community Update"** (`/newswire/article/8971o8789584a4/roleplay-community-update`). Rockstar simultaneously expanded its mods policy to explicitly cover the roleplay creative community.
- Cfx.re at the time: "our partnership with Rockstar Games is focused on our FiveM and RedM platforms. So please, do not ask us about the next GTA!"
- Day-to-day operations were stated to be unchanged.

### Official Rockstar RP-server policy (relevant to the user running an RP server)
Official page: `https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/5I66kExWgligszgMCU3XC1/roleplay-rp-servers`
Prohibited / enforcement priorities:
- Misuse of Rockstar trademarks or IP
- Importing other companies' IP into servers
- Commercial exploitation: selling loot boxes or virtual currency for real money
- Revenue via corporate sponsorships or in-game brand integrations
- Cryptocurrency / crypto assets / NFTs
- Interfering with official online modes
Server monetization is otherwise **permitted** within those limits. Rockstar's framing: third-party RP servers are extensions of community-created experiences they "hope will continue to thrive in a safe and friendly way."
Also relevant: **Cfx.re Creator Platform License Agreement, last updated Jan 12, 2026** — `https://static.cfx.re/platform-license-agreement-12-jan-2026.pdf`

### Current state of the GTA 5 FiveM / RP scene (2026)
- **All-time Steam concurrent peak: 215,265 (April 12, 2026)**; earlier 2026 peak 202,756 (March 15, 2026); crossed 200k for the first time in 2026. Recent typical concurrency ~160k.
- **FiveM for GTA V Enhanced**: announced in development on the Cfx.re forums **March 18, 2026**; **released in early access July 21, 2026**, server-owner-first rollout, general availability "in the coming months."
  - New "Cfx Server": up to **50% less RAM per player**, sync at up to **120 Hz** (was 30/40 Hz)
  - Rebuilt networking stack — **P2P removed, client-server model**
  - Unlocks GTA 5 Enhanced's **ray tracing** and modern renderer
  - Tighter Rockstar Games Launcher integration
  - Server capacity reportedly up to **2,048 players**
- **Bot implication:** Cfx.re forum dev updates + the FiveM changelog are legitimate first-party-ish sources worth monitoring separately from Rockstar Newswire, and are directly actionable for the user's current GTA 5 RP server.

## 8. SOURCE ACCESS NOTES FOR THE BOT (practical)

- **rockstargames.com returns HTTP 403 to programmatic fetches** (tested 2026-08-24 on `/newswire`, the Nov 19 article, and `/newswire.rss`). Expect Cloudflare/bot protection. Plan on realistic browser headers (User-Agent, Accept, Accept-Language), and treat 403 as an expected transient state with backoff — do NOT treat it as "no news".
- A Newswire RSS endpoint is widely cited as `https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire.rss`. It 403'd here, but two open-source projects exist that scrape/relay it to Discord and are worth reading for working header/parse strategy:
  - `https://github.com/NightMean/rockstar-newswire-tracker`
  - `https://github.com/Carbowix/rockstar-newswire`
- **sec.gov also 403'd** direct WebFetch. Use the SEC EDGAR full-text search / submissions JSON API with a proper `User-Agent: name email` header (SEC requires this).
- **take2games.com worked fine** and is the most reliable programmatic primary source:
  - Press releases: `https://www.take2games.com/ir/press-releases/` (note: `ir.take2games.com/news-releases` 302-redirects here)
  - Quarterly earnings: `https://www.take2games.com/ir/quarterly-earnings`
- **businesswire.com timed out** on direct article fetch (60s) but its article URLs are stable and contain the full primary text; consider longer timeouts.
- Newswire article URL shape: `https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/<opaque-id>/<slug>` — the opaque id is not sequential, so you must read a listing/feed rather than guess.
- Official first-party channels to treat as INSTANT-alert tier:
  1. Rockstar Newswire (`rockstargames.com/newswire`)
  2. `rockstargames.com/VI` (the GTA VI site itself — Rockstar edits this page immediately before announcements; a change-hash on this page is a genuine leading indicator)

  5. Take-Two IR press releases + SEC EDGAR 8-K filings
  6. Rockstar official Instagram
  - No official Rockstar Games Bluesky account found.
